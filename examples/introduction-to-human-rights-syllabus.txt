This is illustrative academic material for the LearnSphere application walkthrough.

1. Foundations of Human Rights
Define human rights and examine their historical development, universality, dignity, equality, and the relationship between rights and responsibilities. Compare natural-rights, social-contract, and legal approaches.

2. The Universal Declaration of Human Rights
Study the development and structure of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR). Identify its core principles and explain how the declaration influenced later international and national instruments.

3. Civil and Political Rights
Examine the right to life, liberty and security, freedom of expression, freedom of religion, fair trial guarantees, participation in public affairs, and limits that may apply under law.

4. Economic, Social and Cultural Rights
Review the rights to education, health, work, social security, food, housing, and participation in cultural life. Discuss progressive realization and the duties to respect, protect, and fulfil these rights.

5. International Human Rights Institutions
Map the roles of the United Nations Human Rights Council, treaty bodies, special procedures, regional systems, and national human rights institutions. Compare their mandates and reporting processes.

6. Implementation, Remedies and Accountability
Assess how rights are incorporated into domestic law, monitored, and enforced. Consider access to courts, complaint mechanisms, evidence, remedies, accountability, and practical barriers to implementation.

Assessment prompt: Prepare a short, sourced analysis of one rights issue. State the applicable right, identify a relevant institution or remedy, and explain how the evidence supports your conclusion.
